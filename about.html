We place loan files for a living.

GEMS Profino is a loan facilitator in Chennai. We sit between you and the banks — working out which lender is likely to approve your file, preparing it properly, and following it through to disbursal.

Our story

How this started.

GEMS Profino was registered in Chennai in August 2026. We are new, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise — what we are not new to is the problem we set out to solve. Most people who need a loan approach one bank, get a decision, and never learn why. A file that one lender declines, another sanctions without hesitation. The difference is rarely the borrower. It is which lender saw the file, and how well it was put together before they did.

So we work the other way round. We take your situation first, then decide where the file goes. As an authorised channel partner of Urban Money, we can place an application across more than a hundred banks and NBFCs instead of walking it into one branch and hoping. There are four of us, we answer our own phone, and one person stays with your file from the first call to disbursal.

What we are, precisely

And what we are not.

We are a loan facilitator and an authorised channel partner of Urban Money, a lending service provider. We do not lend money, and we do not decide your application — the bank or NBFC does. What we control is which lender sees your file, how well it is prepared, and how hard it is followed up.

  • We do not set or quote interest rates — the lender does, and we tell you what it says
  • We tell you when we cannot help, rather than wasting your time
  • We will not promise you a decision or a date the lender has not given us
  • Your documents are used for your application and nothing else

The team

Who you will be dealing with.

Four of us, working out of Chennai. One person handles your file from the first call to disbursal, so you are not repeating your situation to a new voice each week — and if you call, you get one of us, not a queue.

  • Six days a week, 9am to 9pm. Closed Sunday.
  • Documents collected from your home or office in Chennai and Tamil Nadu
  • Applications taken across Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana
  • A callback within 30 minutes of your enquiry, during business hours
