Before you ask

Questions we get every day.

Will enquiring here affect my credit score?
No. Talking to us changes nothing on your credit report. A score is only affected when a lender runs a formal credit check, which happens after you decide to apply — and we will tell you before that step.
A bank has already rejected me. Is there any point?
Often, yes. Lenders differ in how they treat irregular income, existing debt, property documentation and past defaults. A rejection at one bank says little about the next. Tell us why you were rejected and we will be straight with you about whether it is worth reapplying.
How long does it take?
It depends on the product. A car loan moves fastest. Home and property loans take longest, because the lender has to value the property and run a legal check on it. We push for a quick decision and tell you where your file actually stands — we will not promise you a date the lender has not given us.
What documents will I need?
It varies by loan and by lender, but almost always: identity and address proof, PAN, six months of bank statements, income proof (salary slips or ITR), and for property loans the title documents. We send you an exact list once we know which lender we are approaching.
Which areas do you cover?
We are based in Chennai and take applications across South India — Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Doorstep document collection is offered in Chennai and Tamil Nadu; elsewhere we handle the file digitally.
