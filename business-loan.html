Who this suits

For traders, manufacturers, professionals and service businesses.

Bank underwriting is built around salaried income, which is exactly why good businesses get declined. What matters is putting your file in front of a lender that reads GST returns and bank statements properly.

  • Working capital for stock, salaries or receivables
  • Expanding to a second location or a larger premises
  • Machinery and equipment purchase
  • Funding a confirmed order you cannot finance from cash flow
  • Consolidating costlier existing business borrowing
  • Professionals — doctors, CAs, architects setting up practice

What you will need

Documents

The exact list depends on the lender we approach. Almost always:

  • PAN and Aadhaar of the proprietor, partners or directors
  • Business registration — GST, Udyam, or Shop & Establishment
  • Last 12 months of current account statements
  • ITR with computation for 2 to 3 years
  • Audited financials, where available
  • GST returns for the last 12 months
  • Existing loan statements, if any

We check every document before submission. Most rejections are filing errors, not credit problems.

Balance transfer

Already servicing a business loan?

A balance transfer moves it to another lender, and for a business the gain is often the cash flow rather than the rate — a longer tenure lowers the monthly outgo even where the rate is similar. Worth looking at if your turnover or filings have improved since you first borrowed, because that is what changes the terms on offer.

  • Bring your sanction letter, loan statements, GST returns and ITR
  • Improved filings since the original loan usually mean better terms
  • We compare the monthly outgo, not just the rate
  • Unsecured and secured business loans are both transferable

Questions

About business loans.

Do I need collateral?
Not always. Several lenders will lend unsecured against your turnover and banking history. Past a certain size, though, offering security — usually property — brings both the rate and the tenure into much better territory. Where that line falls differs by lender, and we will tell you which side of it you are on.
My ITR shows low profit. Can I still borrow?
Often yes. A number of lenders assess banking turnover and GST filings rather than declared profit, precisely because small businesses show conservative profit. This is one of the clearest cases where the choice of lender decides the outcome.
How fast can it be sanctioned?
An unsecured business loan with clean documents moves fastest. Secured loans take longer, because the property has to be valued and legally checked. The lender sets the timeline — we push it along and tell you where the file actually stands.
What if my business is less than two years old?
It narrows the options but does not close them. Some NBFCs lend from one year of vintage, at a higher rate. If you own property, a loan against it is usually the cheaper route for a young business.
Can I prepay without a penalty?
It depends on the lender and whether the rate is fixed or floating. We will tell you the prepayment terms before you sign, not after — it matters a great deal if you expect to clear the loan early.
