What you will need

Documents for a business loan

  • PAN and Aadhaar of the proprietor, partners or directors
  • Business registration — GST, Udyam, or Shop & Establishment
  • Last 12 months of current account statements
  • ITR with computation for 2 years
  • Audited financials, where available
  • GST returns for the last 12 months
  • Existing loan repayment statements, if any

Questions

Business loan FAQs.

Do I need collateral?
Only if your financials require it. Otherwise, no collateral is needed.
My ITR shows low profit. Can I still borrow?
Sometimes. Many lenders look at your turnover and GST filings, not just declared profit.
How fast can it be sanctioned?
It is usually sanctioned the same day or within 24 hours, unless the lender calls for additional documents.
Can I prepay without a penalty?
It depends on the lender. We share the prepayment terms before you sign.
