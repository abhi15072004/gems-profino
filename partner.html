Straight answers

Before you ask.

What are the payout terms?
We agree them with you in writing before you refer your first case, so you know what a closed file is worth to you before you spend any effort on one. It varies by product and by lender, and we would rather tell you the real number for your situation than publish an attractive one here.
Do I need a licence or a registration?
No. You are introducing someone to us, not advising them on credit and not arranging it yourself. GEMS Profino is a loan facilitator and an authorised channel partner of Urban Money; the application is placed under our arrangement, not yours.
What do you need from my client?
To start, only Aadhaar, PAN and six months of bank statements. Anything further depends on the product and the lender, and we will ask for it when it is actually needed rather than up front.
Will you contact my client without me?
We call them about the loan you referred, and nothing else. We do not market other products to them and we do not pass their details on beyond what their application requires — which is set out in our privacy policy.
Is there a minimum number of referrals?
No. Refer one case a year or one a week. There is no target, no joining fee and nothing to buy.
Which areas can I refer from?
Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Document collection in person is offered in Chennai and Tamil Nadu; elsewhere the file is handled digitally.
