How it works

    We call them within 30 minutes during business hours and find out what they actually need.

Straight answers

Before you ask.

What are the payout terms?
We agree them with you in writing before your first case.
Do I need a licence or a registration?
No. You only introduce the client — we handle the rest.
What do you need from my client?
To start, only Aadhaar, PAN and six months of bank statements.
Will you contact my client without me?
Only about the loan you referred, and nothing else.
Is there a minimum number of referrals?
No. Refer one case a year or one a week. There is no target, no joining fee and nothing to buy.
Which areas can I refer from?
Anywhere in South India — Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
